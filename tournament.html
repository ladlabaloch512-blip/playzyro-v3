About This Tournament

The details above (date, format and status) are set by the PlayZyro admin and can be updated at any time. Tournaments labelled Demo or Example are preview data only; real tournament details are added by the admin. Winners from finished tournaments are published on the site.

How to Apply

  1. Log in or sign up for your PlayZyro account.
  2. Have a squad of at least 4 members: create one as captain, or join one with the squad's join code.
  3. Use the Apply button above. The admin reviews applications and assigns squads, and the Room ID and password are published on your dashboard at match time.

Tournament Rules

  • A squad has 4 to 5 players.
  • Each player can be in one active squad at a time.
  • Applications are reviewed by the admin; applying does not guarantee a place.
  • The Room ID and password are published at match time, to assigned squads only.
  • Play fair and keep conduct respectful, as set out in the Terms and Conditions.
